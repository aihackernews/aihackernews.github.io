Submit a news story

Spotted AI news we haven't covered yet? Submissions are handled through GitHub Issues — no account on this site needed, just a free GitHub account. An automated review checks every submission about once an hour.

How to submit

  1. Go to our GitHub issues page: github.com/aihackernews/aihackernews.github.io/issues
  2. Click the green New issue button.
  3. Set the issue title to start with [NEW_POST], followed by the headline of the story, e.g. [NEW_POST] OpenAI launches …
  4. In the description, use one of the two formats below — either share a link, or write the content yourself.
  5. Submit the issue. If it passes review, it appears on the front page and the issue is closed as done.
Open the issues page →

Format A — share a link

Put LINK: on its own line, then the article URL. We'll read the article, write a short summary, and post it with your headline and the link.

Example issue · Open
[NEW_POST] Acme AI releases TurboModel 2, a faster open-weight model
opened by a-reader · just now
Description
LINK:
https://example.com/news/acme-ai-turbomodel-2-release

Format B — write the content yourself

No link? Put CONTENT: on its own line, then type the news in your own words — what happened, who is involved, and when. We'll summarize it and post it with a link back to your issue.

Example issue · Open
[NEW_POST] Local lab open-sources a speech model for 40 languages
opened by a-reader · just now
Description
CONTENT:
Researchers at Example University announced today that they are
open-sourcing WhisperLite-40, a speech recognition model covering
40 languages. The weights and training code are available now, and
the team says it runs on a single consumer GPU. (Write the key facts
in your own words — a few sentences is enough.)

What gets accepted

  • Real AI industry news — model releases, research, products, startups and funding, policy, infrastructure. One specific story per issue.
  • Recent — ideally from the last few days.
  • Not already on the front page — please check for duplicates first (the same announcement from another outlet still counts as a duplicate).
  • No ads or self-promotion. Promotional posts, product pitches and spam are closed without being posted.
  • Keep it suitable for a general audience. No adult or sexual content — including AI tools or apps for creating pornography or sexualized imagery. Submissions like that are closed without being posted.
  • No harmful tools or schemes. We don't post AI tools for crime (malware, phishing, fraud, impersonation deepfakes), scams or "get rich" trading schemes, tools for identifying or tracking private individuals, or content encouraging self-harm or extremism. Straight news about these topics from reputable outlets is fine.
  • You get credited. Accepted stories carry a “by @your-github-handle” line under the story on the front page.

If your submission is closed and you believe it is genuinely new news, reopen the issue and add a comment explaining why — a human will take a second look.
